# 1. What Is TRON Energy?

TRON is a blockchain network that supports smart contracts and digital asset transfers. Accounts on the TRON network can use resources such as Bandwidth and Energy to perform different types of on-chain operations.

Energy is primarily used to execute smart contract operations, including USDT TRC20 transfers.

When a user initiates a USDT TRC20 transfer, the transaction generally consumes a certain amount of Energy. If the account has sufficient Energy, the transaction can use those resources. If Energy is insufficient, the network may consume TRX to cover the corresponding resource costs, subject to the network's rules.

Therefore, understanding how Energy works can help frequent USDT users plan their transaction costs more effectively.

It is important to understand that **Energy and TRX are different types of resources**. Renting Energy does not mean purchasing USDT or increasing your wallet balance. Energy is a network resource used to execute operations on the TRON blockchain.

If an address has never been activated, some Energy rental services may not be able to delegate Energy to it directly. In this case, confirm the address activation requirements and follow the procedures supported by the platform.

# 5. What Should You Know Before Renting Energy Through TRXAPI?

## 1. Energy Rental Does Not Make Every Transfer Completely Free

The main purpose of Energy rental is to provide the network resources needed to execute transactions and help reduce certain transfer costs.

However, transfers may also require other resources, such as Bandwidth. Having sufficient Energy does not necessarily mean that all network fees will be eliminated.

Consider the rental fee, remaining wallet resources, and actual transaction requirements when evaluating the total cost.

## 2. Energy Delegation Has a Specific Duration

Energy rental services may offer per-transaction or time-based plans.

The usage rules can differ between these options:

- Per-transaction plans generally cover a specific resource-use scenario.
- Time-based plans may allow users to use delegated resources repeatedly during the validity period.
- When a time-based plan expires, the delegated resources may be reclaimed.

Always refer to the terms of the selected plan. If you need to make several transfers within a short period, estimate your transaction frequency and compare the total costs of different options.

## 3. Check Your Wallet Status Before Renting Energy

An order may fail if the destination address does not meet the platform's delegation requirements.

Before placing an order, confirm that:

- The address belongs to the correct TRON network.
- The address status meets the platform's requirements.
- The address has been entered correctly.
- The destination address can receive the relevant resources.

For an address that has never been used, review the platform's address activation instructions first. Avoid repeatedly placing orders before confirming the applicable requirements.

## 4. Never Share Your Private Key or Recovery Phrase

A normal Energy delegation process should use the destination wallet's public address, according to the service requirements.

Never provide the following information to unfamiliar websites or customer service representatives:

- Wallet private key
- Recovery phrase or seed phrase
- Wallet password
- Unnecessary authorization information

A public wallet address and a private key serve completely different purposes. The public address identifies the destination for the resources, while private keys and recovery phrases are sensitive information that must remain under your control.

## 5. Compare Actual Costs Before Ordering

Fees can vary between Energy rental plans.

Before renting, compare:

- Cost per rental
- Energy amount
- Delegation duration
- Expected number of transfers
- Estimated cost of consuming TRX directly
- Other potential network fees

Do not focus only on the Energy amount or advertised savings percentage. Long-term users should evaluate overall costs using their actual transaction records.
