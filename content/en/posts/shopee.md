## 5. Shopee Payment Failed: How to Fix It

If your Shopee payment fails, several factors may be responsible.

### Your Card Is Not Supported

Your card issuer may restrict international transactions or payments to certain merchants.

**What to do:** Check whether your card supports the transaction and contact your issuer if necessary.

### Insufficient Balance

Your available balance may not cover the order total, shipping costs, taxes, or applicable transaction fees.

**What to do:** Check your available balance and any pending transactions that may temporarily reduce your funds.

### Incorrect Billing Address

An incorrect billing address or postal code may cause verification problems.

**What to do:** Check your billing information against the records held by your card issuer.

### Transaction Limits or Security Checks

Your issuer or Shopee may require additional verification or restrict a transaction for security reasons.

**What to do:** Follow the verification instructions and contact the relevant support team if the problem continues.

### Payment Pending but Order Not Confirmed

Sometimes, a payment may appear as pending even though the order has not been confirmed.

**What to do:** Check both your Shopee order status and your card transaction history. Contact your card issuer or Shopee support if the transaction remains unresolved.

Do not pay again until you have checked whether the original transaction is still pending.
