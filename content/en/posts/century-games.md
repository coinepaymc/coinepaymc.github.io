## 4. How to Fix Payment Failures

Common causes include:

- Insufficient balance or transaction limits
- Unsupported card types or currencies
- Incorrect billing information
- Issuer restrictions or security checks
- Payments that are still processing

Check the error message and your transaction history first. If a payment is pending, **do not pay again until you confirm its status**.

If payment succeeds but the items do not arrive, save your receipt and order number, then contact the game's official customer support.

## 5. Tips for Safe Top-Ups

- Use official game stores and verified payment pages.
- Check the total price, currency, and fees before paying.
- Never share passwords or verification codes.
- Keep receipts and transaction records.
- Review refund policies before purchasing.
- Confirm pending payment status before retrying.
