## 1. What Is PayPal?

PayPal is an online payment platform that allows users to send and receive payments and manage transaction records, subject to the services available in their country or region.

Depending on local availability and account eligibility, PayPal can be used for:

- **Online shopping:** Buy products from international websites that accept PayPal.
- **Online subscriptions:** Pay for eligible software, digital services, and online subscriptions.
- **International payments:** Send payments to supported merchants and recipients.
- **Cross-border business:** Eligible individuals and businesses can use PayPal to process certain international transactions.

## 3. How to Link a Bank Card to PayPal

If you already have a PayPal account, follow these general steps to try linking a card. Menu names may vary depending on your location and the version of the website or app.

1. Sign in to the official PayPal website or app.
2. Open the Wallet or payment methods section.
3. Select the option to add or link a card.
4. Enter your card number, expiration date, and other required information.
5. Make sure your billing address matches the information registered with your card issuer.
6. Complete any card verification steps requested by PayPal.

If PayPal does not allow you to add your card, the issue may be related to card compatibility, regional restrictions, incorrect information, or security checks.

Always use the official PayPal website and avoid entering your card details on unfamiliar websites.

## 5. What to Do If a PayPal Payment Fails

If your payment fails when using a bank card or virtual card, check the following possible causes.

**1. The card is not supported**

Some virtual or prepaid cards may not work with certain PayPal features. Contact the card issuer to confirm whether your card supports the transaction you want to make.

**2. Insufficient balance**

Check your available balance and allow for potential additional costs, such as service fees, currency conversion fees, or other charges.

**3. Incorrect billing information**

Incorrect card details, a mismatched billing address, or other verification issues may prevent you from linking the card or completing a payment.

**4. Transaction restrictions**

The card issuer may restrict certain international payments, online transactions, or payments to specific merchants. PayPal may also decline a transaction following a security review.

**5. The payment status is unclear**

If a transaction is still processing, or your funds appear to have been deducted but the merchant has not confirmed the order, do not immediately submit the payment again. Check your PayPal transaction history and contact the relevant service provider to confirm the status.

## 6. Tips for Using PayPal and Virtual Cards

To manage online payments more effectively, keep the following tips in mind:

- **Check fees:** Review card service fees, international transaction fees, and currency conversion charges.

- **Avoid duplicate payments:** Verify the transaction status before trying again if the original payment is still pending.
- **Follow platform rules:** Use your PayPal account and virtual card in accordance with the applicable terms of service.

**Q1: Can I link a CoinePay virtual card to PayPal?**

This depends on the card type, issuing provider, and PayPal's requirements in your region. Confirm the card's supported uses with CoinePay before attempting to link it, and follow the instructions displayed by PayPal.

**Q2: Why did PayPal decline my virtual card?**

Possible reasons include an unsupported card type, mismatched billing information, insufficient funds, restrictions imposed by the card issuer, or a failed security review. Check the error message and contact the relevant service provider if necessary.

**Q3: Will I be charged if a PayPal payment fails?**

This depends on the transaction status. Some transactions may fail without a charge, while others may show a temporary authorization or pending status. Check your PayPal transaction history and the information provided by your card issuer. Avoid repeatedly submitting the payment before confirming what happened.

**Q4: Can a PayPal refund be returned to a virtual card?**

Refund handling depends on the original payment method, PayPal's processing rules, and the card issuer's policies. Some eligible refunds may be returned to the original payment card. Contact the relevant service provider to confirm the expected processing time and method.

**Q5: Why can't I make payments even though my card has been linked successfully?**

Successfully linking a card does not mean every transaction will be approved. Check your available balance, transaction restrictions, payment currency, and account status. If the issue continues, contact PayPal support or your card issuer.
