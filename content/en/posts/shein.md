## 5. What to Do If Your SHEIN Payment Fails

If your payment is declined, check the following:

1. **Card details:** Make sure the card number, expiration date, and security code are correct.
2. **Available balance:** Confirm that your balance or credit limit is sufficient.
3. **Transaction permissions:** Check whether online and cross-border transactions are supported.
4. **Billing information:** Use billing details that comply with your card issuer's requirements.
5. **Security verification:** Complete any required verification steps.
6. **Order status:** Check whether an order was created or a payment was deducted before trying again.

If the problem continues, contact SHEIN customer support or your card issuer to identify the cause. Never share your card security code or one-time verification codes with strangers.
