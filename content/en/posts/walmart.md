## 6. Walmart Order Cancellations and Refunds

Sometimes customers submit an order and later discover that it has been canceled, while their card account still shows a pending transaction.

This situation may be related to a payment authorization hold.

Walmart may request a temporary authorization when an order is submitted.

If the order is canceled, the authorization hold is generally released according to the payment processor's and card issuer's procedures.

If the transaction has already been settled, eligible refunds are generally returned to the original payment method.

Customers should:

- Keep the order number;
- Check the payment status;
- Distinguish pending authorizations from completed charges;
- Monitor the original card account for balance updates;
- Contact the card issuer or Walmart support if an issue occurs.
