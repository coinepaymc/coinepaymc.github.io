# **1. What Are TikTok Promote and TikTok Coins?**

## **TikTok Promote**

TikTok Promote is a content promotion feature in the TikTok app. Eligible users can set a promotion goal, audience, and budget for a video, depending on the options available to them. Features and options may vary by region, account, and app version.

## **TikTok Coins**

TikTok Coins are virtual items within the TikTok platform. Users in supported regions can purchase Coins through available payment channels and use them according to TikTok’s rules. Coins are not cash and may not be used to pay for TikTok Promote or advertising fees.

## **TikTok Ads Manager**

TikTok Ads Manager is a platform for creating and managing ad campaigns. Its account requirements, payment process, and billing methods may differ from those for Promote or Coins in the TikTok app.

Before making a payment, confirm whether you are purchasing Coins, promoting a video, or paying for advertising services.

# **3. How to Set Up TikTok Promote**

TikTok’s app interface may change over time. The following steps describe a general process. Follow the instructions shown in the app.

## **Step 1: Log In to Your TikTok Account**

Open the TikTok app and log in. Using an email address or phone number that you can access long-term can make it easier to manage your account and review payment records.

## **Step 2: Choose a Video to Promote**

Go to your profile and select the video you want to promote. Before proceeding, make sure that the video, music, and other materials comply with TikTok’s rules.

## **Step 3: Open Promote**

Open the video’s sharing or more-options menu and look for **Promote**. If you do not see this option, your account, video, region, or app version may not currently support the feature.

## **Step 4: Choose a Promotion Goal and Budget**

Select a promotion goal, audience, duration, and budget from the options shown. Review the amount and currency before submitting your choices.

## **Step 5: Confirm the Payment**

TikTok will display the payment methods currently available to you. Follow the instructions to complete the payment, and keep the order or transaction record. Do not enter your TikTok login or payment details on unofficial pages.

# **5. Common Reasons for TikTok Payment Failures**

## **1. The Card Does Not Support the Transaction**

Some bank cards or virtual cards may restrict international transactions, online payments, or certain types of purchases. Check with the card issuer to confirm which transactions the card supports.

## **2. The Payment Channel or Region Does Not Match**

TikTok’s available payment methods may vary by account region, device operating system, or purchase channel. Check the payment options listed on the current checkout page.

## **3. Billing Information Does Not Match**

A payment may fail if the billing address, postal code, or country does not match the information held by the card issuer. Enter accurate billing details. Do not use a false address.

## **4. The Card Has Insufficient Funds or Available Credit**

Before paying, confirm that the card’s available balance or transaction limit can cover the amount shown on the payment page, including any additional fees that may apply.

## **5. Security Verification Was Not Completed**

TikTok or the card issuer may require additional security checks. If a payment fails, contact TikTok Support or the card issuer through official channels. Do not share verification codes, passwords, or full card details with strangers.
