# 4. Common Reasons Virtual Card Payments Fail on TEMU

- The card does not support international payments in the current region.

- The order does not support the selected payment method.

**Solutions:**

1. Check whether the card is active and valid.

Even if a virtual card is active, a payment may fail if its available balance or credit limit is insufficient.
