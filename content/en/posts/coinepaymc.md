# TRON Network Fee Optimization Guide: Why More Users Are Choosing TRX Energy Rental

As USDT-TRC20 becomes increasingly popular for cross-border payments, digital asset transfers, and blockchain applications, more users are paying attention to a practical question:

**Why do some TRON transactions have lower fees, while others require significantly more TRX?**

The main reason is related to the resource mechanism of the TRON network.

On the TRON network, USDT-TRC20 transfers are not simply asset movements. They involve smart contract execution. During the transaction process, **Energy** is usually required. When a wallet does not have enough Energy, the network may consume TRX to complete the transaction.

For users who only occasionally use the TRON network, this impact may not be significant. However, for users who frequently perform USDT transfers, batch payments, DeFi operations, or other on-chain activities, continuously consuming TRX may increase overall costs.

Therefore, TRX Energy rental has become an option for many users to better manage blockchain transaction costs.

---

# What Is TRX Energy?

The TRON network mainly includes three types of resources:

## Energy

Energy is mainly used for:

- USDT-TRC20 transfers;
- TRC20 token transactions;
- DApp interactions;
- DeFi operations;
- NFT-related activities;
- Smart contract execution.

## Bandwidth

Bandwidth is mainly used for:

- Regular TRX transfers;
- Transaction data processing;
- Basic network operations.

## TRX

When an account does not have enough network resources, TRX may be consumed as a transaction fee.

Since USDT-TRC20 transfers usually involve smart contract execution, Energy is one of the important factors affecting transaction costs.
