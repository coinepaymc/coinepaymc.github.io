# What Is TakeProfitTrader?

TakeProfitTrader follows the common prop firm model.

Instead of giving new traders immediate access to a live funded account, users first complete an evaluation in a simulated environment.

The process usually involves:

- Purchasing a Test Account;
- Reaching the required Profit Target;
- Following Drawdown and Consistency rules;
- Passing the Evaluation;
- Moving to PRO;
- Potentially progressing to PRO+.

Different account sizes come with different profit targets, position limits, and risk rules.

For that reason, traders should not choose an account based only on headline sizes such as 25K, 50K, 100K, or 150K.

# Test, PRO and PRO+ Accounts

## Test Account

The Test Account is the evaluation stage.

Trading takes place in a simulated environment, and traders must meet the platform's performance and risk requirements.

Important rules may include:

- Profit Target;
- Drawdown;
- Consistency;
- Maximum position size;
- Approved products;
- Minimum trading days.

New Test Accounts currently require at least **3 trading days**.

However, trading for three days alone is not enough. The Profit Target and all applicable risk rules must also be satisfied.

---

## PRO Account

After passing the Test, traders can move to PRO.

PRO still uses a:

**Simulated trading environment.**

However, eligible profits can qualify for real payouts.

Current key features include:

- Traders receive **80%** of eligible profits;
- TakeProfitTrader keeps **20%**;
- Day-One Payout is available when requirements are met;
- Daily Payouts are supported;
- A Buffer Zone applies.

For PRO traders, managing Drawdown and Buffer is just as important as generating profits.

---

## PRO+ Account

PRO+ moves traders into a:

**Live-market trading environment.**

Current features include:

- Live-market trading;
- Traders receive **90%** of eligible profits;
- TakeProfitTrader keeps **10%**;
- The same PRO Buffer Zone requirement does not apply;
- Daily Payouts are supported.

Moving from PRO to PRO+ is not simply an automatic upgrade.

Trading performance, consistency, risk management, and overall trading behavior may be considered before a trader moves to this stage.

# Key Evaluation Rules

## Profit Target

Each account size has its own Profit Target.

It is usually better to choose an account based on your trading strategy, average performance, and risk tolerance rather than simply choosing the largest account available.

---

## Drawdown

Drawdown is one of the most important limits in prop firm trading.

If losses reach the maximum permitted level, the account may fail even if it previously generated profits.

Every trade should therefore consider not only potential profit, but also how much risk room remains if the trade loses.

---

## Consistency

Test Accounts also include consistency requirements.

Traders should avoid relying on one unusually large profitable day to complete the entire evaluation.

A more balanced profit distribution may fit the rules better than attempting to pass with one oversized trade.

---

# Buffer Zone and Payouts

Once traders reach PRO, the Buffer Zone becomes important.

The Buffer can be viewed as a level of account protection that should remain in the account.

This means that not all profits are necessarily available for immediate withdrawal.

Before requesting a payout, check:

- Current account balance;
- Buffer level;
- Drawdown;
- Available payout amount;
- Remaining risk room after withdrawal.

One of TakeProfitTrader's notable features is its:

**Day-One & Daily Payout model.**

Once the requirements are met, eligible PRO traders can request payouts without waiting for a traditional monthly payout cycle.

PRO+ also supports Daily Payouts.
