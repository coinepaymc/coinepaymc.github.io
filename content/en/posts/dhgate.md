# 5. Common DHgate Payment Failures and How to Fix Them

## 1. The Virtual Credit Card Is Not Supported

Possible reasons include:

- The card type does not meet the platform's requirements;
- Restrictions apply to the card's issuing region;
- The card does not support international e-commerce transactions;
- The card issuer restricts transactions with certain merchants;
- The checkout page does not accept the card's payment network.

**How to fix it:**

First, confirm whether DHgate accepts that type of card. Then contact the card issuer to verify whether international transactions are enabled.

If the card does not meet the requirements, use another payment method supported by DHgate instead of repeatedly submitting the same failed payment.

## 2. The Billing Address Does Not Match

Incorrect billing information may cause payment authorization to fail.

Common issues include:

- An incorrect cardholder name;
- An incomplete street address;
- An incorrect postal code;
- Inconsistent city or regional information;
- A billing address that does not match the issuer's records.

**How to fix it:**

1. Check the cardholder name registered with the card issuer;
2. Verify the street address and postal code;
3. Confirm the country or region;
4. Enter the actual billing information registered with the issuer;
5. If the payment still fails, contact the issuer to identify the specific reason.

Do not use a fabricated address. Do not enter your shipping address as your billing address unless the two addresses are genuinely the same.

## 3. Insufficient Card Balance

The final cross-border order amount may be higher than the product price shown on the listing.

Additional costs may include:

- Total product price;
- International shipping fees;
- Applicable taxes;
- Currency conversion costs;
- International transaction fees;
- Temporary authorization holds.

If the available balance is insufficient, the transaction may be declined.

**How to fix it:**

- Check the card's available balance;
- Review the final order amount;
- Check for fees charged by the card issuer;
- Confirm whether pending transactions or temporary authorization holds are reducing the available balance.

Make sure sufficient funds are available before trying the payment again.

## 4. International Transaction Permissions Are Disabled

Some bank cards and virtual cards may have restrictions on certain international transactions by default.

Even if a card works for other purchases, it does not necessarily mean that it will work on DHgate.

**How to fix it:**

Contact your card issuer and confirm whether the card supports:

- International online transactions;
- Cross-border e-commerce payments;
- The transaction currency;
- The relevant merchant category.

If the transaction is restricted by the issuer, follow its instructions and select an appropriate alternative payment method.

## 5. The Payment Fails Security Verification

DHgate, payment service providers, or card issuers may review transactions based on their risk assessment policies.

Possible reasons include:

- A newly added payment method;
- Transaction details requiring further verification;
- Incomplete account information;
- An order amount or transaction pattern triggering a review;
- Authorization being declined by the card issuer.

**How to fix it:**

- Check platform notifications and your registered email;
- Make sure your account and order details are accurate and complete;
- Complete any required verification steps;
- Contact the card issuer to check whether the transaction was declined;
- Contact DHgate customer support if necessary.

Avoid repeatedly submitting payments within a short period, and do not use false information to bypass platform security checks.

## 6. The Payment Succeeds but the Order Is Not Confirmed

Sometimes a card transaction appears as charged or authorized, but the DHgate order status does not update immediately.

Possible reasons include:

- The payment is still being processed;
- The issuer has authorized the transaction, but the platform has not yet confirmed the order;
- There is a processing delay between the payment system and the order system;
- The transaction requires additional review.

**How to fix it:**

1. Check the DHgate order page;
2. Review payment confirmation emails;
3. Check your card transaction records;
4. Keep the order number and payment evidence;
5. If the status does not update after a reasonable period, contact DHgate customer support or your card issuer.

Do not rush to pay again before confirming the status of the original transaction. This can help prevent duplicate payments or multiple pending authorizations.

## 7. Order Cancellation or Refund Delays

After an order is canceled, your card statement may still show a pending amount.

This may be caused by a temporary authorization hold rather than a completed charge.

If the authorization is reversed, the funds are generally released according to the card issuer's processing rules.

If the payment was fully charged and the order qualifies for a refund, the refund must be processed according to the platform's and payment provider's procedures.

Recommended actions:

- Keep the order number;
- Check the cancellation reason;
- Determine whether the transaction is a pending authorization or a completed charge;
- Check the refund status on the platform;
- Monitor the original payment method;
- Contact DHgate customer support or your card issuer if anything appears unusual.

Refund processing times vary depending on the payment method, card issuer, and processing procedures.
