## 2. Can You Pay for Waffo AI with a Virtual Card?

A virtual card may work if Waffo's checkout accepts its card network and the issuer permits the transaction.

Before subscribing, check these requirements:

- **Supported card network:** Confirm whether your virtual card uses Visa, Mastercard, or another supported network.
- **International payments:** Make sure international online transactions are enabled.
- **Recurring payments:** If you choose a monthly or annual subscription, your card must support recurring charges.
- **Available balance:** Keep enough funds to cover the subscription price and any applicable taxes or conversion fees.
- **Verification:** Be prepared to complete any required security checks, such as 3D Secure authentication.

Virtual card acceptance is not guaranteed. The result depends on the checkout configuration, card issuer, billing details, and transaction risk checks.

## 4. What If Your Virtual Card Is Declined?

If your payment fails, check the following:

- **Insufficient balance:** Add enough funds to cover the total amount.
- **International payments disabled:** Check your card settings or contact the issuer.
- **Recurring payments unsupported:** Confirm that the card allows subscription charges.
- **Incorrect billing information:** Review the card details and billing address.
- **Security verification failed:** Complete any required verification and retry if appropriate.
- **Merchant restrictions:** Some virtual cards or transactions may not be supported by the payment provider.

If the payment continues to fail, contact the card issuer or Waffo support for assistance. Avoid repeatedly submitting the same declined transaction.
