## 5. How to Fix AliExpress Payment Issues

If your bank card or virtual card payment fails, check the following common causes.

**1. The card is not supported**

Some virtual or prepaid cards may not work on certain online shopping platforms. Confirm that your card supports international online transactions and meets AliExpress's current payment requirements.

**2. Insufficient available balance**

Check whether your card has enough available funds to cover the order. Remember to account for possible currency conversion fees, transaction fees, and other charges.

**3. Incorrect card details**

Incorrect card numbers, expiration dates, or other required information may prevent a payment from going through. Carefully review the details before trying again.

**4. Transaction restrictions**

The card issuer may restrict certain international transactions, currencies, or merchants. AliExpress may also decline a payment following its security checks.

**5. Billing information mismatch**

If your billing address or other details do not match the information held by the card issuer, the payment may fail. Provide accurate information according to the platform's and issuer's requirements.

**6. Unclear payment status**

If the payment is still processing, or your card appears to have been charged but the order has not been confirmed, check your AliExpress order status and card transaction history.

Do not immediately submit the payment again. First, confirm whether the original transaction succeeded, remains pending, or requires assistance from customer support or the card issuer.
