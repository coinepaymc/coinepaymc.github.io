# What Is Topstep?

Topstep is a prop firm focused mainly on futures traders.

New users do not immediately receive a live funded account. Instead, they first purchase a Trading Combine and trade in a simulated environment while meeting the platform's performance and risk requirements.

The process generally includes:

- Purchasing a Trading Combine
- Reaching the Profit Target
- Following the Maximum Loss Limit
- Meeting the Consistency Target
- Passing the evaluation
- Receiving an Express Funded Account
- Potentially progressing to a Live Funded Account

Topstep currently offers common Trading Combine account sizes such as:

- 50K
- 100K
- 150K

Different account sizes come with different targets, position limits, and risk parameters.

# Trading Combine Rules

The Trading Combine is Topstep's evaluation stage.

Although traders use live market data, the account itself is simulated.

The most important rules include Profit Target, Consistency Target, and Maximum Loss Limit.

## Profit Target

Each account size has its own Profit Target.

Traders should choose an account based on their strategy, average performance, and risk tolerance rather than simply choosing the largest account.

---

## Consistency Target

Topstep currently uses a **55% Consistency Target** in the Trading Combine.

In simple terms, your best trading day should not represent too large a percentage of your total profits.

If one day produces an unusually large portion of your profits, you may need to continue trading until your results meet the consistency requirement.

---

## Maximum Loss Limit

The Maximum Loss Limit, or MLL, is one of the most important risk rules.

If the account reaches or falls below this limit, the Trading Combine may fail.

Even if you are close to the Profit Target, oversized positions can still cause the account to violate the loss limit.

For this reason, Position Size and risk control are usually more important than trying to pass as quickly as possible.

# What Is an Express Funded Account?

After passing the Trading Combine, traders can receive an **Express Funded Account (XFA)**.

It is important to understand that:

**XFA is still a simulated funded account.**

However, eligible traders can request real payouts according to Topstep's rules.

Once you move into XFA, you no longer continue paying the Trading Combine monthly subscription for that account.

Topstep currently allows traders to hold multiple active XFA accounts, subject to its account limits and rules.

---

# How Do XFA Payouts Work?

Topstep offers different payout paths for Express Funded Accounts.

## Standard Path

The Standard path generally requires traders to complete qualifying Winning Days before requesting a payout.

A qualifying Winning Day must meet the minimum net profit requirement defined by Topstep.

Once the requirements are met, traders may request a portion of their eligible account balance, subject to the payout limits for that account.

---

## Consistency Path

Topstep also offers a Consistency-based payout option.

This route generally focuses on:

- Minimum trading days
- Consistency requirements
- Eligible account balance
- Payout limits

Under the current profit split structure:

**Traders receive 90% of eligible profits.**

**Topstep keeps 10%.**

The exact payout amount depends on the account size, payout path, and the eligible amount shown in the trader dashboard.

---

# What Is a Live Funded Account?

The **Live Funded Account (LFA)** is the stage where traders use real Topstep capital in the live market.

Moving from XFA to LFA is not simply an automatic upgrade.

Topstep may review factors such as:

- Trading performance
- Risk management
- Consistency
- Trading behavior
- Overall account history

Live Funded Account payout rules are different from XFA rules.

Traders may first need to complete a certain number of Benchmark Trading Days before unlocking more flexible payout options.

The long-term progression is therefore:

**Build a consistent record → Earn XFA payouts → Potentially move to a Live Funded Account**

# How Much Does Topstep Cost?

Topstep's Trading Combine uses a monthly subscription model.

Topstep offers different pricing paths, including options with a lower monthly fee and an activation fee after passing, as well as plans with a higher monthly fee but no separate XFA activation fee.

Common Trading Combine account sizes include:

### 50K Account

Standard pricing typically has a lower monthly cost.

A No Activation Fee option may also be available at a higher monthly price.

### 100K Account

The monthly price is higher than the 50K plan, with both Standard and No Activation Fee options available.

### 150K Account

This is the largest common Trading Combine size and generally has the highest monthly subscription cost.

Pricing can change, so check Topstep's current checkout page before purchasing.

It is also worth considering how long you expect to stay in the evaluation and whether an activation fee would apply after passing.

## Incorrect Billing Information

Double-check:

- Cardholder name
- Billing Address
- Postal Code
- Country or region

---

## Insufficient Balance

Keeping only the exact purchase amount on the card may not always be enough.

Exchange-rate changes, taxes, authorization holds, or card-related fees may require additional available balance.

---

## Too Many Payment Attempts

If a payment has already been declined, avoid repeatedly submitting the transaction or switching cards several times within a short period.

Check the reason first, then try again.
