## 1. What Is Etsy?

Etsy is an online marketplace that connects buyers with independent sellers offering a wide range of unique products. Unlike conventional retail platforms, Etsy is particularly known for handmade goods, vintage products, personalized items, and craft supplies.

Popular product categories on Etsy include:

- Handmade jewelry and accessories
- Personalized gifts and custom-made products
- Home décor and furniture
- Clothing, bags, and fashion accessories
- Vintage collectibles
- Digital downloads and creative resources
- Art, craft supplies, and DIY materials

Etsy sellers operate independently, so product availability, shipping methods, delivery times, and return policies may vary from one shop to another.

Before placing an order, review the seller's product description, shipping information, estimated delivery date, and return policy.

## 2. What Payment Methods Does Etsy Accept?

Etsy supports several payment methods, although the options available to you depend on your location, the seller, and the checkout process.

Common payment options may include:

- Credit and debit cards, such as Visa and Mastercard
- PayPal, where available
- Etsy gift cards and Etsy credits, subject to applicable restrictions
- Other locally supported payment methods displayed at checkout

Not every payment method is available in every country or for every transaction.

If you want to use a CoinePay virtual card, first check whether the card operates on a payment network accepted by Etsy and whether your card is eligible for the transaction.

Keep in mind that Etsy does not guarantee acceptance of every virtual card. A card may be declined because of issuer restrictions, billing-address mismatches, insufficient funds, security checks, or other payment-processing requirements.

## 3. How to Add a Bank Card on Etsy

If you want to pay for an Etsy order with a credit or debit card, follow these general steps.

### Step 1: Sign In to Etsy

Visit the official Etsy website and sign in to your account. If you do not have an account, follow Etsy's registration process.

### Step 2: Choose a Product

Browse the marketplace and select the item you want to purchase.

Check the product details, available variations, shipping costs, estimated delivery date, and seller policies before continuing.

### Step 3: Proceed to Checkout

Add the product to your cart and proceed to checkout.

Confirm your shipping address and review the order total, including applicable taxes and shipping fees.

### Step 4: Select a Card Payment Option

If card payments are available, choose the relevant credit or debit card option displayed during checkout.

Enter the requested card details, such as:

- Card number
- Expiration date
- Security code (CVV or CVC)
- Cardholder name, if requested
- Billing address

The exact fields may vary depending on the checkout interface and payment processor.

### Step 5: Review and Submit Your Order

Carefully verify your payment and shipping information before placing the order.

If Etsy requests additional authentication or verification, follow the instructions displayed on the official website.

If your card is declined, review the error message and check your card details, available balance, billing address, and issuer restrictions before trying again.

## 5. How to Troubleshoot Etsy Payment Failures

If Etsy declines your CoinePay virtual card or another payment card, several factors may be responsible.

### 1. Check Your Available Balance

Make sure your card has enough funds to cover the order total, including shipping, taxes, and any applicable fees.

If your balance is insufficient, add funds according to the card issuer's procedures before trying again.

### 2. Verify Your Card Details

Check the card number, expiration date, security code, and any other information you entered.

Even a small error may prevent a transaction from being processed.

### 3. Confirm Your Billing Address

Make sure the billing information you provide matches the information associated with your card, as required by the issuer.

Incorrect or inconsistent billing details may trigger a payment decline.

### 4. Check Card Restrictions

Some virtual cards may have restrictions on international transactions, certain merchant categories, online purchases, or specific currencies.

Review the terms provided by your card issuer to determine whether your intended purchase is permitted.

### 5. Check Etsy's Payment Requirements

Etsy and its payment processors may apply security checks or other transaction requirements.

If a payment is declined, read the on-screen message and follow any instructions provided by Etsy or your card issuer.

### 6. Contact Customer Support

If you have checked your balance and card details but the payment still fails, contact the relevant card issuer or Etsy Support.

Provide the transaction details requested by the official support channel, but never share your full card security code or password in an unsecured message.

### 7. Avoid Repeated Unsuccessful Attempts

Repeatedly submitting a declined payment may trigger additional security checks.

Instead, identify the likely cause of the problem before making another attempt. If necessary, use another payment method offered by Etsy.

## 6. Important Tips for Shopping on Etsy with a Virtual Card

To make your shopping experience smoother, keep the following recommendations in mind.

- **Check seller information:** Review shop ratings, customer feedback, product descriptions, and shipping policies before placing an order.
- **Confirm the total cost:** Include shipping, taxes, currency conversion, and potential transaction fees when calculating your budget.
- **Use accurate information:** Enter your billing and shipping details carefully and follow the requirements of both Etsy and your card issuer.
- **Protect your payment details:** Never share your card number, security code, account password, or verification codes with untrusted parties.
- **Keep your order records:** Save order confirmations, payment receipts, and relevant communication with the seller.
- **Understand refund procedures:** Refunds and cancellations are subject to Etsy's policies, the seller's terms, and the card issuer's processing procedures.
- **Have an alternative payment method:** A virtual card may not be accepted for every transaction, so consider another payment option available at checkout.

A virtual card can be useful for online purchases, but it is important to understand its restrictions and to use it in accordance with the card issuer's terms.
