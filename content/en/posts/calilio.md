# What If Your Calilio Payment Fails?

## International Payments Are Disabled

Check whether your card supports:

- International Payments;
- Online Payments;
- Foreign Currency Transactions.

## Incorrect Billing Information

Double-check:

- Cardholder Name;
- Billing Address;
- Postal Code;
- Country or region.

## Insufficient Balance

Calilio charges may include more than just the subscription plan.

You may also need to pay for:

- Virtual numbers;
- Calling usage;
- SMS usage;
- Wallet top-ups.

Keeping some extra balance can help avoid payment failures.

## Repeated Payment Attempts

If a payment has already been declined, avoid submitting the same transaction repeatedly within a short period.

Check the card status and Billing information first, then try again.
